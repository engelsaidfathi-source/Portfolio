Embedded Systems · Course A403

Fire Fighting Robot

An autonomous robot that detects a fire, drives to it, stops at a safe distance and extinguishes it.

Course
Embedded Systems (A403), The British University in Egypt
Supervisors
Dr. Ahmed Abdalsalam and Dr. Hesham Auda
Built with
Arduino Uno, flame sensors, ultrasonic sensor, L298N, relay, pump, servo

The course poster. Select it to view full size.

How it works

  1. DetectThree IR flame sensors (left, centre, right) pick up the fire's heat and infrared light.
  2. ApproachThe Arduino steers the wheels toward the fire through the L298N motor driver.
  3. Stop safelyThe ultrasonic sensor sees the fire as an obstacle and halts the robot 30 cm away.
  4. ExtinguishA relay switches on the water pump while a servo sweeps the hose from 0° to 180°.

Features

Fire detection

Three IR flame sensors give accurate direction detection.

Fire tracking

Moves toward the source automatically using DC motors.

Obstacle detection

Ultrasonic sensing keeps operation safe.

Safe stopping

Stops 30 cm before reaching the fire.

Fire extinguishing

A relay activates the water pump.

Wide coverage

A servo rotates the hose from 0° to 180°.

Full automation

Controlled entirely by the Arduino, with no human intervention.

Components

  • Arduino Uno
  • 3 × flame sensors
  • Ultrasonic sensor
  • L298N motor driver
  • Relay module
  • Water pump
  • DC motors
